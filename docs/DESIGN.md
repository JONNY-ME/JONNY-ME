# The Signal Foundry

An original engineering illustration for Yohannes Melese's profile. The title names the artwork, not a company or an additional professional identity.

## Composition

The YM monogram is drawn as routed copper paths. A teal input waveform leads into the mark, and its output branches into a neural network, software brackets, and a chip. These represent three disciplines; they do not claim the featured projects form one deployed system. The small registration labels and dot field borrow from engineering drawings. The diagrams explain structures, not benchmark measurements.

The desktop hero uses an 840 × 310 canvas. The compact hero uses 360 × 300 and removes detailed branch diagrams to preserve the monogram and readable labels. Project diagrams have separate 840 × 190 and 360 × 180 compositions. Essential explanations are also present in ordinary Markdown and alt text.

Dark colors: ink `#101820`, ivory `#F4EFE6`, copper `#D99A63`, teal `#75C4BC`. The light palette uses ivory surfaces with ink text, copper `#8D4C27`, and teal `#236B66`. The palettes share geometry. Fonts use system monospace stacks inside the artwork; the profile's text remains native GitHub typography.

The hero trace draws once over 4.5 seconds; one junction glows and settles by 4.8 seconds. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the animation is disabled and the final geometry remains visible. No interaction or essential content depends on animation. Illustrations contain no scripts, embedded HTML, bitmap assets, external fonts, or network calls.
